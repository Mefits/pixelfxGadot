# Physics Lab — first physical body prototype
Open I in the main menu. A/D move, W or Space jump, hold E near a top corner to grip, V debug, P pause, X quarter-speed, R reset, 1/2 decrease/increase tail segments, 3/4 decrease/increase mass, left-drag nearby physical point to apply a force. Tail/mass changes reset the body for controlled comparison, not live evolution yet.
Core physical_body.gd: fixed 120 Hz position-based integration, inverse-mass weighted XPBD-style distance constraints with compliance, static AABB circle contacts, velocity/friction, center of mass, debug contacts and constraint error. physical_creature.gd: two main chunks with optional tail, force-driven movement, jump/coyote window, simple top-corner grip. The skin follows simulated coordinates. No animation clips drive the body. This is an independent prototype inspired by the general chunk-based approach, not a port of Rain World code.
Limits: static rectangles only, no general continuous collision detection, no self collision, no deformable terrain, no procedural walking limbs yet; grip is a compliant pull rather than a full locomotion system. No autonomous climbing or attachment to vertical walls. Direction steering is an approximate muscle force. Tail and mass tuning are prototype defaults. Test both light and heavy bodies, wall/ledge contact, turn, jump and falls; report qualitative feel separately from numerical tests.
Next: procedural feet, support selection and reach constraints; then author pose targets that act as motor goals rather than overwriting physical coordinates.
