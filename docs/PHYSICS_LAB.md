# Physics Lab — first physical body prototype
Open I in the main menu. A/D move, W or Space jump, automatic grip when moving toward a reachable top corner, Q toggles procedural legs, V debug, P pause, X quarter-speed, R reset, 1/2 decrease/increase tail segments, 3/4 decrease/increase mass, left-drag nearby physical point to apply a force. Tail/mass changes reset the body for controlled comparison, not live evolution yet.
Core physical_body.gd: fixed 120 Hz position-based integration, inverse-mass weighted XPBD-style distance constraints with compliance, static AABB circle contacts, velocity/friction, center of mass, debug contacts and constraint error. physical_creature.gd: two main chunks with optional tail, force-driven movement, jump/coyote window, simple top-corner grip. The skin follows simulated coordinates. No animation clips drive the body. This is an independent prototype inspired by the general chunk-based approach, not a port of Rain World code.
Limits: static rectangles only, no general continuous collision detection, no self collision, no deformable terrain, procedural walking limbs are a first-pass spring support and analytical IK, grip is a compliant pull rather than a full climbing system. No general wall crawling or ceiling attachment. Direction steering is an approximate muscle force. Tail and mass tuning are prototype defaults. Test both light and heavy bodies, wall/ledge contact, turn, jump and falls; report qualitative feel separately from numerical tests.
Next: procedural feet, support selection and reach constraints; then author pose targets that act as motor goals rather than overwriting physical coordinates.

## v0.4 — procedural locomotion
Four feet search horizontal rectangle surfaces independently, maintain world-space contacts and use a diagonal swing gait. Planted feet apply bounded vertical support forces to the two physical chunks. Two-link IK is a visual representation, not separate simulated limb masses. Horizontal locomotion retains the original body motor. Q toggles legs without resetting. Auto ledge pull requires movement toward the top corner, head within reach of the top, and space for the body. Reverse to cancel. No pulling up from the base of a tall wall. Jump and tail tuning intentionally deferred. No claim of Rain World fidelity yet.

## v0.5 — surface adaptation
Keep the predictable body motor as the primary input controller. Feet provide support and adapt procedurally rather than determine input speed. Up/Down requests wall ascent/descent only next to a reachable vertical face; stopping holds position, moving away or jumping detaches. Reaching the top hands off to the existing automatic ledge pull. C disables wall adhesion, Q disables legs, M toggles a wall/low-clearance course. Floor support height is reduced under low ceilings, without changing collision radii. No ceiling walking or free-form slopes yet; static rectangles only. Narrow passage behavior requires manual testing; the wall tests cover ascent, hold, descent, jump, unreachable surfaces and top transition.

## v0.6 — first hybrid authored motion
F starts a claw attack. The normalized intention track controls front limb target, trunk inclination, motor speed and rear support height. Other limbs retain procedural gait. The attacking limb relinquishes its contact, solves two-link IK with static rectangle segment collision and swept tip checks, and returns to procedural control at the end. Fixed 120 Hz active window and per-target hit IDs are independent of render cadence. The red training target loses HP only from a reachable hit; G moves it to the mouse and restores HP. No VFX by design. Attack is disabled during wall hold or ledge pull; gravity remains active in air. A shorter two-segment, lighter tail is default; 1/2 still adjust length. This is a hand-authored motion track in code, not yet integrated into the Pose Workshop. Collision checks are static rectangle approximations; no dynamic creature collisions or simulated limb masses. If no clear IK pose exists, the attacking limb is not rendered and cannot hit. Next: improve collision-aware recovery/pose blending and expose intention tracks in the editor before evolution.
