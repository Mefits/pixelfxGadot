# Original effect workshop
This is separate from exact upstream ports. First original composition: Rift Collapse (custom/rift_collapse.gd). A shrinking broken orbit with inward filaments, a central release, asymmetric branching and expanding energy rings, cooling debris and translucent smoke. Three art-direction presets and arbitrary seed. No copied authored effect choreography; the shared verified PixelFX primitives do the raster work.
Open K from main lab; 1/2/3 choose preset, Space replay, P pause, slider scrub, N seed, B background, A backend. The effect is pure time driven and seekable. effect_player.gd is reusable for native pure effects. Prepare before combat, then play/seek/pause without disk access. A custom world trajectory/collision requires further design.
For custom effects do not fabricate an upstream golden reference: verify deterministic replay, nonempty phases, empty terminal frame and exact backend agreement; archive CPU contact sheets and perform visual iteration in Godot. The contact sheet is a buffer composite, not a GPU screenshot. Human artistic approval remains separate from correctness.
