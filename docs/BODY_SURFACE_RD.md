# 0.56 — mesh/implicit hybrid decision (Sol)
Owner rejected 0.55c: exposed shoulders, long neck, square torn shorts, feet, flat fabric; also original body too rounded at attachments. Do not treat 0.55c as accepted art.
- Godot Polygon2D bone weights: https://docs.godotengine.org/en/4.4/tutorials/animation/2d_skeletons.html — editable topology and per-vertex influences; direct CanvasItem path cannot share our CPU per-pixel z buffer.
- libigl https://libigl.github.io/tutorial/ — LBS and DQS; joint collapse and twist remain issues. 2D projection plus arbitrary depth cannot recover full 3D.
- Vaillant et al. SIGGRAPH 2013 https://rodolphe-vaillant.fr/entry/31/implicit-skinning-real-time-skin-deformation-with-contact-modeli — correct geometric skinning using implicit surfaces to model contact and preserve detail. Not a requirement to implement the paper wholesale.
Decision: 2.5D explicit triangle front surfaces with common depth/ownership/finish; pose-space rows with shared attachment vertices as cheapest test before general weighted mesh. Retain analytic primitives as optional anatomy/detail and later joint corrections. Not SDF-only, not full 3D, not yet generic bind-pose LBS. Prototype tests seam-free shared edge raster, arbitrary winding, z crossing, invalid inputs, existing oracle; compare neutral and existing run/guard/jab/kick. Watch folds, overlaps, depth and gaps; if failures systemic, Astra task: stable indexed topology, weighted deformation with local corrective constraints and topology-aware visibility, native batch API, no art. No Astra dispatch until owner decides.
